# QuantumFlow QF-2025 User & Troubleshooting Guide

## Warranty Information
The QuantumFlow QF-2025 comes with a standard 3-year limited manufacturer warranty from the date of original purchase. This warranty covers defect in materials, manufacturing flaws, and internal hardware components under normal residential operating conditions.

To claim warranty support:
1. Provide valid proof of purchase (invoice or digital order receipt).
2. Register the device serial number at support.quantumflow.com.
3. Extended warranty coverage up to 5 years is available via QuantumCare+ within 30 days of purchase.

## Power & Wi-Fi Troubleshooting

### Power Connection Issues
If the QuantumFlow QF-2025 fails to turn on or experiences unexpected power resets:
- **Check Circuit Breaker:** Ensure the 120V main circuit breaker supplying power to the outlet has not tripped.
- **Inspect Power Cable:** Check the 24V AC transformer harness for loose wire connection nuts or pinched insulation.
- **Hard Reset:** Unplug the main power supply, wait 30 seconds, and reconnect. The green LED power indicator on the front panel should illuminate solid green.

### Wi-Fi Connection Troubleshooting
If the device loses connection to the local network or offline status appears in the app:
- **2.4 GHz Network Band Required:** The QF-2025 requires a dedicated 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi band. Ensure your router broadcasts 2.4 GHz separately from 5 GHz.
- **Wi-Fi Module Reset:** Hold the "Reset" button on the underside of the QF-2025 control module for 10 seconds until the Wi-Fi LED blinks rapidly in neon blue.
- **Network Security:** Ensure your router uses WPA2-PSK or WPA3-Personal encryption. Enterprise 802.1X networks are not supported.

## Zenith Smart Thermostat Setup Guide

1. Turn off main HVAC power at the breaker panel before starting installation.
2. Remove old thermostat faceplate and label existing C, R, W, Y, and G wires.
3. Mount the Zenith wallplate using the built-in bubble level and included mounting screws.
4. Attach wire leads firmly into terminal blocks matching letter designations.
5. Snap Zenith Smart Thermostat module onto the wallplate and restore main HVAC power.
6. Follow the on-screen wizard to pair with the QuantumFlow mobile application via Bluetooth.
